About the Doctor

Dr. Shashank Agrawal

Consultant Urologist & Uro-Oncologist, Sindhu Hospitals

MBBS MS DrNB (Urology) MNAMS

With 9 years of practice, Dr. Agrawal trained in urology at Muljibhai Patel Urological Hospital and completed a competitively selected, two-year fellowship in Uro-Oncology & Robotic Surgery at Amrita Institute of Medical Sciences, Kochi. He is a certified Console Surgeon (Intuitive Surgical Systems) for advanced robotic procedures.

His practice focuses on the management of urological malignancies — including robotic prostatectomy, robotic partial nephrectomy and robotic cystectomy — alongside open, laparoscopic and endoscopic surgery for complex urological cases. He has authored over 20 peer-reviewed articles in national and international urology journals.

Areas of expertise: Urological malignancies, laparoscopic & endoscopic surgery, advanced robotic surgery (prostatectomy, partial nephrectomy, cystectomy), general urology & andrology.
