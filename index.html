Surgical Expertise

Procedures & Surgeries

Select a procedure to learn how it works and what to expect.

Endourology

Retrograde Intrarenal Surgery (RIRS)

A flexible scope is passed through the natural urinary passage to reach the kidney, where stones are fragmented using laser energy — with no external incision required.

Laser Therapy

Holmium Laser Lithotripsy

Holmium laser energy breaks stones into fine fragments while sparing the surrounding tissue, allowing for a quicker, incision-free recovery.

Endourology

Percutaneous Renal Surgery (PCNL)

A small puncture in the back provides direct access to the kidney for removing larger or complex stones in a single procedure.

Non-Invasive

Lithotripsy (ESWL)

Focused shock waves delivered from outside the body break stones into fragments small enough to pass naturally.

Prostate Surgery

TURP

Trans-urethral resection relieves obstruction caused by an enlarged prostate, restoring normal urine flow without any external cut.

Transplant Surgery

Kidney Transplantation

End-to-end transplant care — from donor evaluation through surgery and long-term immunosuppression follow-up.

Minimally Invasive

Laparoscopic Urology

Key-hole surgical techniques applied across a range of kidney and urological procedures for a faster return to daily life.
