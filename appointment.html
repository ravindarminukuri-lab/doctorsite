Before You Visit

Appointment FAQs

Walk-ins are accommodated where possible, but booking ahead is recommended to avoid waiting, especially for first-time consultations. Urgent cases are always prioritised regardless of whether an appointment was booked in advance.

Yes, video consultations are available for follow-up visits and select initial consultations where an in-person examination isn't immediately required. Mention this preference in the message field when booking.

Most requests are confirmed by phone or email within a few working hours. For same-day or urgent requests, calling the clinic directly is the fastest way to secure a slot.

You can reschedule or cancel by calling the clinic ahead of your appointment time. Giving as much notice as possible helps free up the slot for another patient.

Several major insurance providers are accepted; bring your insurance card and any pre-authorisation documents to your visit. Contact the front desk beforehand to confirm coverage for your specific plan.
