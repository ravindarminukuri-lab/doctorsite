10 Warning Signs You Should Never Ignore

Urology · 12 Aug 2026

Many urological conditions are highly treatable when caught early, but symptoms are sometimes brushed aside. Here are ten signs that are worth a prompt conversation with a urologist.

  • Blood in the urine, even a single episode
  • Persistent lower back or flank pain
  • Burning or pain during urination
  • A sudden change in urinary frequency or urgency
  • Difficulty starting or maintaining a urine stream
  • A lump or swelling in the testicle
  • Erectile difficulties lasting more than a few weeks
  • Recurrent urinary tract infections
  • Unexplained weight loss with urinary symptoms
  • Difficulty conceiving after a year of trying

Why Early Evaluation Matters

Many of these symptoms have simple, treatable explanations — but a few can signal more serious underlying conditions, including cancers, that respond best to early treatment. A thorough evaluation gives you clarity either way.

What to Expect at Your First Visit

A first consultation usually involves a detailed history, a focused examination, and any tests needed to reach a clear diagnosis — explained in plain language at every step.
