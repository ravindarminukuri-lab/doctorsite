Understanding Prostate Health

Men's Health · 14 Jun 2026

The prostate is a small gland that plays a role in male reproductive health, and it naturally changes as men age. Understanding these changes helps you know what's normal and when to seek an evaluation.

Common Age-Related Changes

Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH), a non-cancerous enlargement of the prostate, is extremely common after age 50. It can press on the urethra and cause urinary symptoms such as a weak stream or frequent nighttime urination.

When to See a Urologist

  • Difficulty starting or stopping urination
  • Frequent urination, especially at night
  • Blood in urine or semen
  • A family history of prostate cancer

Screening and Early Detection

Routine PSA testing and a physical examination, generally starting around age 50 (earlier with a family history), allow early detection of concerning changes — when treatment tends to be most effective.

Treatment Approaches

Depending on the diagnosis, treatment can range from watchful waiting and medication to minimally invasive or surgical procedures. A tailored plan considers your symptoms, overall health and personal preferences.
