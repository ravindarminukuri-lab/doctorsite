Understanding Prostate Cancer Treatment

Understanding the Condition

What Is Prostate Cancer?

Prostate cancer develops in the prostate gland and ranges widely in behaviour, from very slow-growing disease suited to monitoring, to more aggressive cancer needing active treatment.

  • Often no symptoms at all in early, localised disease
  • A weak urine stream or difficulty urinating
  • Blood in the urine or semen
Our Clinical Approach

How We Guide Your Treatment

A raised PSA blood test, digital rectal exam and MRI-guided or standard biopsy are used together to diagnose and grade prostate cancer.

  • PSA blood test and digital rectal examination
  • MRI imaging to identify areas of concern
  • MRI-guided or standard biopsy to confirm and grade the cancer
Advanced Techniques

Treatment Options

Treatment ranges from monitoring to robotic surgery, chosen according to your cancer's risk category.

  • Active surveillance for low-risk, localised disease
  • Robotic radical prostatectomy with nerve-sparing technique
  • Combined radiation and hormonal therapy for higher-risk disease
Optimal Results

Recovery & Outcomes

Recovery after robotic radical prostatectomy typically involves a short hospital stay, a period of catheter use, and a gradual return to normal activity over several weeks.

  • Short hospital stay for most robotic procedures
  • Gradual recovery of continence over weeks to months
  • Regular PSA monitoring after treatment
