Why Choose Our Testicular Cancer Treatment?

Testicular cancer is one of the most curable cancers in medicine, and Dr. Agrawal's approach reflects that — thorough, evidence-based treatment paired with genuine attention to your long-term fertility and hormonal health, not just cancer control.

Confirming the Diagnosis

Orchidectomy — surgical removal of the affected testicle — both treats the primary tumour and confirms its exact type, guiding any further treatment.

When RPLND Is Needed

For select patients, retroperitoneal lymph node dissection is performed with nerve-sparing technique to protect fertility-related function.

Understanding Testicular Cancer Treatment

Understanding the Condition

What Is Testicular Cancer?

Testicular cancer begins in the cells of the testicle and typically affects younger men. It has some of the highest cure rates of any cancer when treated promptly.

  • A lump or swelling in the testicle, usually painless
  • A feeling of heaviness in the scrotum
  • A dull ache in the lower abdomen or groin
Our Clinical Approach

How We Guide Your Treatment

Diagnosis is based on physical examination, scrotal ultrasound and tumour marker blood tests, followed by orchidectomy to confirm the tumour type.

  • Physical examination and scrotal ultrasound
  • Tumour marker blood tests
  • Orchidectomy to confirm the tumour type
Advanced Techniques

Treatment Options

Further treatment, if needed, is chosen based on the tumour type and stage confirmed after orchidectomy.

  • Surveillance or further treatment based on tumour type and stage
  • Retroperitoneal lymph node dissection (RPLND) for select cases
  • Fertility-conscious treatment planning throughout
Optimal Results

Recovery & Outcomes

Recovery after orchidectomy is usually quick, with most patients returning to normal activity within a couple of weeks.

  • Quick recovery after orchidectomy for most patients
  • Longer, monitored recovery if RPLND is required
  • Ongoing surveillance with tumour markers and imaging
