Penile Cancer

Organ-preserving and reconstructive treatment approaches, including robotic management of lymph nodes.

Penile cancer is uncommon, but early, precise treatment matters both for cancer control and for preserving function and appearance. Dr. Agrawal offers organ-preserving and reconstructive approaches wherever the disease allows.

Organ-Preserving Priority

Function- and appearance-preserving techniques used whenever oncologically safe.

Robotic Lymph Node Surgery

Minimally invasive management of groin lymph nodes when involved.

Sensitive, Private Care

Consultations handled with the privacy and sensitivity this diagnosis deserves.

Signs to Watch For

  • A persistent sore, lump or ulcer on the penis
  • Changes in skin colour or thickening
  • Discharge or bleeding
  • A palpable lump in the groin

Diagnosis

Diagnosis is confirmed with a biopsy of the lesion, along with imaging and examination of the groin lymph nodes to check whether the cancer has spread.

Treatment Options

  • Organ-sparing surgical techniques whenever oncologically safe
  • Robotic or open inguinal lymph node dissection for groin involvement
  • Management of locally advanced disease with multimodal treatment
  • Reconstructive options to support functional and cosmetic outcomes

Recovery & Outlook

Recovery depends on the extent of surgery required. Organ-preserving procedures generally allow a quicker return to normal activity, while lymph node surgery recovery is supported with close wound care and monitoring for swelling in the leg or groin.

  • Wound care guidance provided for surgical sites
  • Monitoring for groin or leg swelling after lymph node surgery
  • Structured follow-up to watch for recurrence
