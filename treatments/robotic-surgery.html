Robotic Surgery

Precision robotic surgery for urological cancers through tiny incisions, with faster recovery and better preservation of function.

Robotic-assisted surgery lets Dr. Agrawal operate through a few small incisions with enhanced 3D visualisation, precision and range of motion. For many urological cancers, this can mean less blood loss, less pain and a quicker return to normal activity, without compromising on cancer-control outcomes compared with open surgery.

Small Incisions

Surgery performed through a few small ports instead of one large incision.

3D Precision Vision

Magnified visualisation supports more accurate, controlled dissection.

Faster Recovery

Many patients mobilise sooner and return to routine activity more quickly.

Same Cancer Control

Oncological outcomes comparable to conventional open surgery.

Where Robotic Surgery Is Used

  • Kidney tumours — robotic partial or radical nephrectomy
  • Bladder cancer — robotic radical cystectomy with reconstruction
  • Prostate cancer — robotic radical prostatectomy
  • Upper tract tumours — robotic radical nephroureterectomy
  • Complex reconstructive and lymph node procedures

Recovery & Outlook

Recovery after robotic surgery varies by procedure, but many patients benefit from less post-operative pain, a shorter hospital stay and an earlier return to daily activities compared with open surgery. Structured follow-up continues afterward to monitor healing and long-term outcomes.

  • Typically a shorter hospital stay than open surgery
  • Gradual, guided return to normal activity
  • Scheduled follow-up to track recovery and outcomes
