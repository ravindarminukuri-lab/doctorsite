Meet the Specialist
MBBS MS DrNB (Urology) MNAMS

Dr. Shashank Agrawal is a Consultant Urologist and Uro-Oncologist at Sindhu Hospitals, Hyderabad, with 9 years of experience in the field. He completed his urology training at the Muljibhai Patel Urological Hospital and went on to pursue an intensive, two-year fellowship in Uro-Oncology and Robotic Surgery at the Amrita Institute of Medical Sciences, Kochi — a program he was selected for through a highly competitive process.

He is a certified Console Surgeon accredited by Intuitive Surgical Systems, specialising in advanced robotic procedures. His expertise spans the management of urological malignancies, including Robotic Prostatectomy for prostate cancer, Robotic Partial Nephrectomy for kidney cancer, and Robotic Cystectomy for bladder cancer. He is equally proficient in open, laparoscopic and endoscopic surgery for a wide range of urological conditions, including complex cases.

An Accomplished Academician

Dr. Agrawal has authored over 20 peer-reviewed articles in leading national and international urology journals and serves as an active reviewer for several respected publications in the field. His contributions to uro-oncology have earned him multiple accolades and recognition, and he remains dedicated to ensuring every patient receives the highest standard of care.

Areas of Expertise

  • Management of urological malignancies — kidney, bladder, prostate, penile and testicular cancers
  • Robotic Radical Prostatectomy, Robotic Partial & Radical Nephrectomy, Robotic Radical Cystectomy
  • Robotic Radical Nephroureterectomy for upper tract urothelial carcinoma (UTUC)
  • Retroperitoneal and inguinal lymph node dissection
  • Second opinions on urological cancer diagnosis and treatment planning
