About Dr. Arvind Rao

Consultant Urologist, Andrologist & Kidney Transplant Surgeon — 20+ years of focused, minimally invasive urological care.

M.S., M.Ch (Urology) Fellowship, Renal Transplantation Member, Urological Society
Consultant Profile

Two decades of specialist urology practice

Dr. Arvind Rao is a Consultant Urologist, Andrologist and Kidney Transplant Surgeon based in Hyderabad. His practice centres on endourology, laparoscopic urology and renal transplantation, with a consistent focus on minimally invasive techniques that reduce hospital stay, scarring and time away from daily life.

Over more than 20 years in consultant practice, he has managed a broad range of urological conditions — from routine stone disease to complex reconstructive and transplant surgery — while keeping communication with patients and families clear at every stage of treatment.
