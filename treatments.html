Ask Us Directly

Every treatment plan starts with a consultation and, where needed, imaging or pathology review. Dr. Agrawal reviews your history, diagnosis and reports before recommending the most suitable, least invasive option that effectively treats your condition.

Yes. Robotic surgery is a well-established, minimally invasive approach that typically means smaller incisions, less pain and a faster recovery than open surgery, with cancer-control outcomes comparable to conventional surgery when performed by a trained surgeon.

Recovery time depends on the specific procedure — robotic surgery often allows a shorter hospital stay and quicker return to normal activity than open surgery, but every case is different. Your surgeon will give you a personalised recovery timeline.

Wherever the cancer's size, stage and location allow it, Dr. Agrawal prioritises organ-preserving and nerve-sparing techniques — such as partial nephrectomy or nerve-sparing prostatectomy — without compromising cancer control.

Yes. Second opinions are common and encouraged in cancer care. Dr. Agrawal reviews existing imaging, pathology and recommendations to offer an independent, evidence-based view before you decide.
